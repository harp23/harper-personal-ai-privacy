Privacy Policy — Harper Personal AI

Last updated: October 2026

Harper Personal AI is a personal household software tool. This privacy policy applies only to this application.

What this app is

Harper Personal AI is a private, self-hosted assistant built and used by a single household for its own personal use. It is not a public product, is not distributed to other users, and no one outside this household has access to it.

What data this app accesses

This app requests read-only access to one Google Calendar (the household's personal/family calendar), using the calendar.events.readonly scope. It can view calendar event details — titles, times, locations — on that one calendar. It cannot create, edit, delete, or otherwise modify any calendar or event, and it does not request access to any other Google data (email, contacts, files, or other calendars).

How this data is used

Calendar data is used solely to answer the household's own questions about its own schedule (for example, "what's on my calendar this week"). It is processed locally on the household's own hardware and is never sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party.

Data storage

The OAuth credentials used to access this data are stored locally, on hardware owned and controlled by the household, and are not transmitted to or stored by any third-party service. No calendar data is retained in any external database.

Data retention and deletion

Calendar data is read on demand and is not permanently stored by this app. Access can be revoked at any time by the account owner at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
